Kelvin Zhang's Personal Conversations Sync Script

A private utility operated by Kelvin Zhang to maintain a text-only archive of his conversations from messaging apps in his own Google Drive and Google Sheets.

This is not a public service. There is no public registration, download, or access to the archive. This page provides information about the Google account connection.
