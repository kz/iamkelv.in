Kelvin Zhang's Personal Conversations Sync Script

Privacy information

Last updated: 9 October 2026.

Google access. The script requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. It creates and updates the owner's archive files and accesses files made available to this application. It does not request access to all files in the Google account.

Data processed. The archive includes conversation text, sender labels, timestamps, and information needed to represent edits and deletions. Media attachments are not downloaded or archived; text captions and simple attachment placeholders may be retained.

Storage. Archive content is stored in the owner's Google Drive and Google Sheets. Encrypted working state is stored in a private Google Cloud Storage bucket. OAuth credentials and encryption keys are stored in Google Cloud Secret Manager. Local and cloud workers process private working data while running.

Purpose and sharing. Google user data is used to operate this personal archive. The script does not sell that data or use it for advertising. Google services process the data as part of storage and API operation. Any separate sharing or connection of the archive to another application is controlled by the owner and is outside this script's automatic sync function.

Retention and deletion. The owner controls retention of the archive and can delete its files. The script retains current and previous encrypted working snapshots. Older working snapshots are deleted after a new checkpoint is committed, with soft deletion disabled. Previously soft-deleted copies may remain until their original seven-day retention expires. Administrative infrastructure backups retain version history and seven-day soft deletion. Google Sheets revision history, service backups, and separately connected applications may retain additional copies. Revoking OAuth access stops future Google access but does not automatically delete the archive.

Account control. The owner can revoke the application's connection in Google Account settings and stop its scheduled executions in Google Cloud. The script has no public user accounts and does not accept other users' data.
